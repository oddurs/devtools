Checked on 2026-10-09 against every live address the new pages link to.
Thirty-one answer and render. One is up but broken, one runs only at a
platform's default domain, a dozen are linked from nowhere on GitHub, and
three that should be sites are not deployed at all.

An address on a page is a promise that outlives the deploy that made it. A
renamed repository, a preview URL or a default subdomain is the kind of
address that breaks without anyone noticing.
