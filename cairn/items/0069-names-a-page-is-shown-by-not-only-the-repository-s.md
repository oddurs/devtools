## What changed

A tool may carry a `title` (what it calls itself) and a `short` (for the rail).
The repository name stays the address, so no link breaks, and search finds
either.

Only the awkward ones were touched: hyphenated working titles and names that
differ from the project's own. chess-strat is The Cow, herdr-namesync is
namesync, ag-test is agt, nether-c is Nether C, cartogram is Inflatable World,
and the essays and books carry their real titles. Single-word lowercase names
are the house style and were left alone.
