## What changed

Every project was read again from its README by a set of agents that were
not shown the old structure, and a separate pass proposed a taxonomy from
what they found. Its shelves were better than the old ones and were taken;
its terminal sections were not, and the README research showed a cluster it
missed (agents).

Shelves say what a thing is, which is what its page will be. Sections say
what it is about.

- **Terminal**: System, Code, Agents, Projects, Machines, News and notes,
  Science, Art and life
- **Desk**: Apps, Look and sound
- **Play**: Games, Toys
- **Look**: Maps, Simulations, Instruments
- **Read**: Explainers, Research, Books and courses
- **Build**: Web, Languages and libraries

Web used to hold both what builds a site and sites that are the point in
themselves; the second kind is now on Play, Look or Read. Pieces is gone:
it was a drawer for whatever did not fit. Six shelf names sit in the rail as
two rows of three.
