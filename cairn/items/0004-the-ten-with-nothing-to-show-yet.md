Ten tools with no story at all. This is the wave that gates the rest.

andy, rigor, jerk, brevity, polkadot, knit, fontina, clackson, turborust and
triblenka have no screens and no recording. Today their pages carry a typed
session; when that goes (0006) they carry nothing.

So this wave is the one that gates the session's removal, and the hard part of
most of these items is not the story but the fixture: a believable machine,
repository or feed for the tool to be photographed against.
