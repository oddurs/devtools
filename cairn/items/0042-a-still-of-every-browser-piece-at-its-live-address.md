## What changed

Thirty-three posters in `static/media/posters/`, 1600×1000, taken from each
piece's live address in headless Chromium with software WebGL. A poster is
captioned with the address it came from, and its alt text is the tool's line.

einstein was taken with its broken asset paths rewritten (see its hosting
item), and yggdrasil with its welcome tour dismissed. bushy was left out: with
no camera it is a permission error, and a face is the whole point.
