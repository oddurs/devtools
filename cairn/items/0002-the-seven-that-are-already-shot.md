Seven pages that need nothing but their words finishing.

poptop, quarry, yoghurt, hackney, gummyworm, starward and tsi have their four
beats and a recording, taken in the current palette. What is left on them is
the line, the longer paragraph, and four captions each.

The cheapest wave, and the one that sets the standard the other seventeen are
held to.
